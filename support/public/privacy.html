Privacy

This static support service provides model metadata only. The retired diagnostic upload endpoint and online video helper are no longer offered. Previous reports were accepted only with consent under the retention period disclosed at upload; this update does not publish or reprocess those reports.

Model downloads and visits to this site contact their hosts, which may process ordinary request and network information. No account or internet connection is required for generation after model installation.

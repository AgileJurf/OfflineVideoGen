Diagnostics you choose to share

Uploading is optional and requires reviewing one report and explicitly choosing Send. Nothing is queued or uploaded automatically. Declining does not affect generation.

Reports contain app/OS/model versions, session outcome, elapsed time, sampled peak memory, thermal state, memory-warning counts, and model partition timing/failure counts. They exclude prompts, seeds, prompt hashes, videos, tensors, file paths, personal files, device identifiers and raw error messages.

Reports are stored privately in the developer’s Vercel account with a 30-day expiration. Vercel processes expirations daily, so deletion may take up to about 32 days. Only people with access to that account can read them. There is no public report-reading endpoint. The service accepts at most one report per two-hour interval across all users; if unavailable, local reports remain in the app.

Vercel processes network metadata, including your IP address and request headers, when handling a request. The application does not store those in diagnostic reports or log report bodies. Model hosts receive network metadata when downloading models. No online support requests occur during video inference.

Optional configuration checks contact this service only when you choose Check for updates. Cached configuration and bundled defaults remain usable offline.

Optional 24 FPS video smoothing

The app can send one selected video directly to the community 1inkusFace/RIFE Space on Hugging Face to create interpolated frames. This requires enabling online support, previewing the exact clip, and explicitly consenting to that upload. Every retry or new clip requires another review and consent. Enabling support or finishing generation never uploads a clip automatically.

The full selected MP4 and network metadata such as your IP address reach Hugging Face and its community Space operator. Prompts, seeds, library metadata, diagnostics and other files are not attached. No Hugging Face account, browser cookies, access tokens or paid credits are used. Anonymous shared free quota may be unavailable; there is no paid fallback.

This service is operated by a third party. Its upload and output cache retention period is not guaranteed. Local Frames cannot confirm remote deletion, and cancelling locally may not stop remote processing. Only approve clips you are comfortable sharing with that service. Review Hugging Face’s privacy policy.

Vercel does not receive or proxy these videos. The original remains unchanged on your iPhone; a successful 24 FPS result is saved as a separate local copy. Offline generation, playback and export do not require online smoothing.
